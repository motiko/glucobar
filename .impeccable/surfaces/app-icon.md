# Surface: App icon and menu bar glyph

Mode: persuade (an identity mark; it must say "glucose gauge for the Mac" at a glance).

## Direction contract

THESIS: GlucoBar is an instrument you glance at, so the icon is a dial: a graphite gauge whose target range is a wider, brighter arc and whose single green needle rests inside it. It refuses the category default (a wavy CGM line on a blue gradient, blood drops) and any clinical or Abbott look.

OWN-WORLD: graphite squircle (#3a3d44 to #16171b), white in-range arc, grey out-of-range arcs, faint tick marks, one green needle and hub (#5ee7a8 to #1fb57a), a rounded 112 below. Range reads by stroke width, not colour alone.

STORY: the Patient sees a calm instrument that reads "in range" and recognises the app that sits by the clock.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 220-degree dial centred high, needle at about 1 o'clock inside the white arc, hub at the optical centre, 112 under the hub. Below 64 px the 112 drops and the dial carries it.

FORM: grounded, safer-register lineup, chosen card 1 of 3 (Instrument Gauge); seed:e9300ff68025b19f.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
